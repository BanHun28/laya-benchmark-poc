## Key readings (hand-written, added after the tables were generated; rerunning the script removes this section)

1. **No variant to promote to router_v2. v1 is kept.** The variants with the same contract as v1 (v2a 0.536, v2c 0.540, v2d 0.516) are all below v1's 0.560 (Δ −0.020\~−0.044, McNemar p 0.14\~0.53, not significant). Per the pre-set rule (§9), none is adopted. If one had to be named, the dev-best v2 is v2c. But the v2c run at the default budget had its options truncated at 30 tokens, so it did not measure "descriptions with examples attached". It is 1 row (0.004) away from v2a, effectively a tie. The conclusion of wording round 1 of 05 P1 step 7 is "the description wording did not raise dev accuracy".
2. **Sharpening the descriptions (v2a) only moved where the errors fall.** Improvements: weather 1/8→4/8, news 1/7→3/7, lists 0/7→3/7. Losses: media recall 20/37→12/37 (play 16/25→10/25), qa 8/16→5/16, general 11→9, which offset the gains. The wrong answers absorbed into smart_home did not decrease (41→44). "(오디오 재생·음량은 제외)" (excluding audio playback and volume) was added to the smart_home description, but all 6 audio rows are still wrong (3 of them to smart_home). That the exclusion wording does not take effect points the same way as H7 (zero-shot failure on negation). This was not tested directly (an estimate).
3. **Dropping general (v2b) and the coarse 5-class option (v2e) are worse than handling the 8-option run at the decode stage (§2).** On the same 220 rows, v2b 0.545 is below the v2a general mask 0.591 (p=0.053), which in turn is below the v1 general mask 0.632 (p=0.0019 vs v2b). v2e 0.536 also falls short of the v2a coarse sum 0.645 (p=0.0088) and the v1 coarse sum 0.682 (p=3.8e-4). The recall of v2e's daily_life ("음식·요리·추천·교통·이동", food·cooking·recommendations·transport·getting around) is 0.322. Only 6/32 of the transport scenario were right, and the wrong answers went mainly to smart_home (daily_life→smart_home ×26). Compressing the description lost information. If coarse routing is needed, summing the v1 probabilities per class is better than changing the schema.
4. **A confidence gate does not filter out the gold general rows (§3).** Under v2b, 23 of the 30 general rows went to smart_home, with a median confidence of 0.76. The AUROC for identifying general by low confidence is 0.574 (v2e 0.642). Catching 73% of general with T=0.9 requires holding back 38% of the correct non-general answers. At the same T, v1, which keeps the general option, is ahead on both the ROUTE count (108 vs 97) and accuracy (0.815 vs 0.763). The option "remove the general option + replace it with a gate" is rejected.
5. **The smart_home sink comes from meaning more than from position.** Under v2d (general first, smart_home last), smart_home predictions went 70→60 (gold 30) and general predictions 33→20, with accuracy −0.020 (p=0.50). In the C1 permutation mean too, smart_home predictions go 66→58.6, still a 2× overprediction. Conversely, the first slot is a disadvantage. Putting general first reduced its predictions by 13, and productivity, first in v1, has 12 predictions in the original order and 21.8 on the permutation mean.
6. **Inserted examples do not fit the default budget (v2c 418 tokens > 240).** Raising the budget to 512 makes it much worse (v2c_h512 0.408, p=2.6e-7, 114 smart_home predictions). This diagnostic run cannot separate whether the cause is the head length (407 tokens, possibly outside the training distribution) or the example content. In v2c@256 the wrong answers absorbed into smart_home halved to 22, but in this run the smart_home option was cut at "…켜고 끄" (mid-way through "turn on and off"), dropping the exclusion wording and "설정 변경" (change settings), so the effects are mixed.
7. **Implications.** This round shows no path to close the 24pt to G1 (0.80) with wording alone. The remaining paths are P4 (fine-tuning) or redefining the contract (e.g., routing by coarse sums of the v1 probabilities). If round 2, which 05 P1 step 7 allows, is run, the candidate is a small change that adjusts only the other options without lengthening v1's smart_home description. The expected effect is small (an estimate).

## 3. The 30 gold general rows: does a confidence gate catch them when there is no general option?

Gate tables: below T → FALLBACK. `general caught` = share of gold general rows with conf < T, `non-general correct held back` = among the 220 scored rows, correct but conf < T (coverage lost), `non-general wrong held back` = among the wrong answers, conf < T (errors filtered out), `ROUTE accuracy` = accuracy of the non-general rows with conf ≥ T. AUROC = probability that the conf of a gold general row is lower than that of a non-general row (0.5 = no separation).

## 9. Applying the selection rule (rule set before the runs)
