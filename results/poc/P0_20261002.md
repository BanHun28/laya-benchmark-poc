## Anomalies / notes
- Verbatim warning. 1 in the english probe run (as expected): `.venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/laya/router.py:594: RuntimeWarning: laya: this checkpoint ships invalid temperatures or values outside [0.5, 5]; using choice:11+=0.10058280825614929 -> 0.5. Treat confidence from the affected entries as uncalibrated.` 0 on the multilingual paths (the instrumented SDK runs, recorded with `warnings.simplefilter("always")`, the CLI and the probe). The rest of stderr is only the HF hub `Fetching 5 files: 100%` progress bar (cache hit). No tokenizer-patch warning or CPU fallback.
- The `rl_agent_config.json` of the multilingual checkpoint has `temperature: [1.0, 1.0, 1.0]`, `temperature_by_options: {}`. That is, answer_confidence is an uncalibrated softmax (the docstring at .venv/.../laya/confidence.py:20 (not included in the public repository) also says "`laya-multilingual` ships with no fitted temperatures at all"). The initial T values of 05 §5 are applied to uncalibrated probabilities.
- The 4 multilingual noul have a combined accuracy equal to the always-no base rate (0.7750), and needs_llm is below its base rate (0.633 vs 0.667). frustration MAE 1.084 is worse than the always-level-0 baseline (0.700). In this probe, criteria-less noul and score added almost no information zero-shot.
- On the P0 sentence all 3 noul were confidently wrong (is_urgent 0.064, refund_requested 0.067, churn_risk 0.025, all with answer_confidence 0.93 or higher) and went through the 0.7 gate as `passed`. `--preset triage`, which has the same noul definitions, gave the same values (the per-question rows are independent of each other).
- On the same sentence, intent was cancellation 0.41 (abstained) with Korean criteria (v1) and refund 0.997 with English criteria (preset). n=1, but it is a signal of criteria-language sensitivity (a target for Track B3 / the `--descriptions` ablation).
- The 7-row MPS call was not faster in fp16 (73.83 ms) than in fp32 (69.81 ms). The 5-row threshold at 02:330 was confirmed (dtype_for(7)=fp16), but in this shape fp16 brings no gain.
- The "about 13 s" Metal compilation on the first MPS call (05:354, 02:329) was not reproduced (first call of a new process 172.70 ms, whole verbatim-snippet process 4.79 s). The OS Metal shader cache may have been warm from the smoke-test runs earlier today (not verified; cold conditions were not controlled).
- The criteria-less noul of the english control varied strongly with the state (needs_llm p 0.02\~1.00, positive 17/30; churn_risk positive 6/30; is_urgent 3/30; refund_requested 2/30). This differs from the premise at 05:239, 05:277 and 04:81 ("english says no regardless of the state", source third_party/laya/README.md:1448-1451, not included in the public repository; recreate it by cloning https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya at commit 4aa6761). It is an observation under the condition of Korean input to a checkpoint that cannot read Hangul.
- In 3 of the 5 negation rows (4, 7, 10, 11, 29), intent went to the negated target (row 7 "해지할 생각은 없는데요" → cancellation 0.48, row 11 "환불은 필요 없고요" → refund 0.998 + refund_requested 0.65, row 29 "해지 문의는 아니고요" → cancellation 0.98). churn_risk was correctly negative on all 5 negation rows, but its overall positive rate is only 0.067, so this is no evidence of discrimination. refund_requested 0.51 on row 10 is a false positive on something that was not the negated target. An early signal for H7 (01:268).
- frustration level-0 selection rate 0/30 (expected 18/30) agrees with "multilingual almost never picks the first level (#131)" at 05:276. Scores range over 1.01\~2.37, so the `frustration >= 2.5` rule never fired in this probe, and together with 0 is_urgent positives this leaves ESCALATE at 0.
- The `decide()` of 05:302-304 checks FORWARD_LLM before urgent, so an inquiry that is urgent and also needs a generated answer becomes FORWARD_LLM instead of ESCALATE (in the reference column based on expected labels, rows 2·6·27 are such cases; 27 is urgent through frustration 3). An item to check in the policy design.
- The probe labels are ad-hoc labels by the operator of this run alone (1 person), not a label policy (06 §4). The `#` header on the first line of the JSONL says not to reuse them. The labeling criteria for needs_llm and complexity are themselves subjective (05:278), so these figures are especially weak.
- Environment state: AC power, battery 100 %, `pmset` powermode 2. Concurrent load was not cleared (latency is a reference value for this machine only).
- No re-runs. On the same device, the answers of all 10 measured calls were identical to the first call (`answers_identical_across_calls=true`).
