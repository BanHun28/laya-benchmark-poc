Checked on 2026-10-12. The repository's code and documents are licensed under Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`). Data files derived from public datasets, and the other third-party material listed below, keep the license of their source; the Apache-2.0 grant does not cover them.

- **Rows.** One row per source: the source with the revision pinned by the builders, its license as stated at the primary source, the conditions for the copies here, and the tracked paths that contain material from it (text, labels or tokenized items).
- **Changes.** Files derived from a dataset were changed: rows were sampled, reformatted into laya-evals JSONL or Jev playground forms, tokenized for training, and combined with question wording and option descriptions written for this project or taken from Laya.
- **Attribution.** The attribution required by the CC BY, CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC licenses is given in this table.
- **ShareAlike and NonCommercial.** Files derived from KLUE, K-MHaS and 3i4K (CC BY-SA 4.0) and from BoolQ (CC BY-SA 3.0) are distributed under their source license. Files derived from the support-ticket dataset (CC BY-NC 4.0) may not be used commercially. `results/D/states_ko.json` mixes several Korean sources and carries the terms of each.
- **Excerpts.** Documents, reports and a few result files quote single dataset items as examples; these excerpts keep their source license. Result files that hold only item ids, labels, scores and model outputs contain no source text.
- **Unclear terms.** "Unclear" means that no license or terms of use were found at the sources checked; those files are included because no term found forbids redistribution.
- **Build-time notes.** The `license` fields in the `MANIFEST.json` files and in the header line of each data file were written when the sets were built and are marked as to be verified; where they differ from this table, this table applies.
